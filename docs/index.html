NEAT, from scratch, to 95% landings in wind, turbulence and past a boulder

An evolved neural network (NEAT: the network's weights and its wiring evolve) flies Gymnasium's Lunar Lander in strong gusty wind (power 15), turbulence (1.5) and past a boulder floating on the way down. Goal: ≥ 95% landed between the flags on 1,000 unseen flights, confirmed on a second fresh 1,000 (the user set 95% on 1 November; the brief said 98%). The last project reached it with PPO (98.8% / 98.4%); two projects ago NEAT stalled at ~85%. This time NEAT starts clean (random genomes, no copied pilot), with a curriculum, a network big enough (32 hidden nodes to start, growing and pruning from there) and a fairer boulder layout (v3) that is passable by construction.
